Billing & wallet

Two screens that decide whether the product looks trustworthy. The live versions quote ₹0/mo against a 999-seat meter, which is not a pricing page anyone believes.

49Billing & plan /billingA5 · config + meters

The live screen prices the current plan twice in one viewport — ₹0/mo in the plan card and ₹85,000/mo on the card badged current. Here the plan is named once, priced once and dated. Rivergrove is on Growth, which is the plan its four AI employees actually fit, so the seat meter can carry a real limit instead of the 999 sentinel. Every meter states what happens when it fills; the three tier cards keep all eleven audited features, and the two most prominent buttons are no longer downgrades.

50bWallet — low balance /walletA5 · state

The state the sitemap asks for: AI usage is billed from the wallet, so low and empty need a design. The banner says what happens at zero in the same words the daily cap uses — finish the thread, start nothing new, hand the queue to a person — so the product behaves one way whichever limit it hits. That behaviour is a design proposal to confirm; the live wallet does not say. Pack prices are the live ones.
