Settings

Nineteen screens across the product share this one shell — nav, tab strip, section cards, save state. Nine are drawn here because between them they cover every sub-shape the rest are made from: field stack, record list, status grid, definition table, audit trail, credential list and form with a live preview. The remaining ten are content in the same frame. Three of these screens were the group's worst defects — a field model defined twice in two vocabularies, a fill statistic that could not be true, and a workspace key printed in the clear three times.
