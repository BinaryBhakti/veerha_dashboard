Guest pages

Ten pages a customer opens from a WhatsApp message, on a phone, having never heard of Veerha. Archetype A12: no app shell, one column, and the property’s branding rather than ours — the guest has a relationship with Rivergrove Retreat, not with the platform that sent the link. These are the only screens in the set built at 390 first and checked at desktop afterwards, which is the right way round for the only pages whose traffic is almost entirely mobile.

Four of the ten are drawn against live captures of the current pages; the rest are designed from the product’s own field definitions, and each frame note says which it is. Two routes the contract names do not resolve in production at all (/s/:token/review, /book/:token) — that is a finding, not a gap in the research.

68Public enquiry form /f/:slugA12

The field set is not invented — it is the eleven qualification questions from Settings → Fields, in the order the AI asks them, and Ages of the children is the one field that appears only when Children is 1 or more. Everything above the fold is three fields, because a form that opens on thirteen gets closed.

Evidence: Field set from the live /f/ capture and settings/fields; layout designed.

74Online check-in /s/:token/check-inA12

75Preferences & consent /pref/:tokenA12

Eleven fields sitting at 0% filled in the dashboard, which is the whole problem: nobody fills a form that does not say what it buys them. So every question carries the reason it is asked, the two sensitive ones are labelled as sensitive to the guest and not just internally, and consent is three separate answers because agreeing to a booking confirmation is not agreeing to marketing.

Evidence: The eleven guest questions are verbatim from settings/fields; the Sensitive and PII labels are the product’s own. Layout designed.

77Terminal states — thank-you, expired, actioned all tokenizedA12

Every tokenized link ends in one of these three, and the live app gets two of them wrong by showing the marketing site instead. The rule: say what happened, say what the link does now, and give one way forward. Never a dead end, and never a redirect to a homepage the guest did not ask for.

Evidence: The expired wording is verbatim from the live page — it is the one piece of this copy the product already had right. Thank-you and actioned are designed.
