Campaigns

Six screens, and the module where the archetypes are most likely to collapse into each other — three of them are builders. They stay distinct because they are building different things: the landing page has a palette and picks a block to edit, the creative studio has no palette and its inspector is always live, and the campaign builder is a stepper that writes one offer into three channels at once.

Lead nurture

Sent automatically after a lead arrives, and stopped the moment they reply or the deal closes.

Three of the four steps have never sent

WhatsApp only accepts free text within 24 hours of the customer’s last message. Every step after day 1 falls outside that window, so it needs an approved template and does not have one. 36 sends have been skipped since 1 September.
