Commerce — the E-commerce edition

Counter and Orders exist only in Veerha for E-commerce. A Hotels workspace redirects both routes, so these are designed from the client’s sitemap (flow F6) and not observed. They use a separate fictional shop, Riverbank Organics, so the two editions never share a fixture.

95Orders /ordersA1 · index

Designed from the sitemap: fulfilment, address, payments, receipts, with a badge for orders needing action. The two statuses that matter — payment and fulfilment — are separate columns, because “paid but not packed” and “packed but unpaid” are different problems. Not observed: the route redirects in a Hotels workspace.

94Counter — the till /counterA10 · operational workspace

Designed from the sitemap’s flow F6: search, cart, offer code, optional customer, payment, record. The customer field says it is optional on its face, as the brief’s rule requires; a walk-in needs nothing. Out-of-stock items stay visible but dimmed, so the counter can say no without searching.
