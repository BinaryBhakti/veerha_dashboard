Leads

The densest module in the product and the most reused. A1 — the index workbench — is the layout behind nine screens; A3 — the record drawer over it — is behind three more. Get these two right and a third of the product is composition.

1 Leads index /leads A1 · the template behind 9 screens

Header and actions, then the six scope tiles that double as filters, then the filter bar, then the table. The Veerha's read column is the signature move — a one-line conclusion per row, so a user can triage without opening anything. Selected rows take a tint and a 2px left rule; they are never lifted into a card.

2dLead — action dialogs dialogsopened from buttons on this module’s screens

Every action on a lead that needs a moment of input opens one of these, from the row menu, the drawer’s action row or the recommended step. Each states what will happen before it happens — the sitemap’s rule — and every one is reversible from its toast, except Delete, which says so and offers Archive instead.

logcall · Call, Log call, Call now
Log a call with Arjun Mehta

Calls happen on your phone; this records what was said.

Veerha adds this to the lead’s activity and reads it before the next reply.
Book the next step
Tomorrow 11:00 · call · with Anita
followup · Follow-up, Book a follow-up, Remind me
Book a follow-up with Arjun Mehta

It appears in Tasks and on the Calendar, and Veerha will not chase him while it is pending.

meeting · Meeting, Schedule
Schedule a meeting with Arjun Mehta

Only times free on your calendar and inside working hours are offered.

Send the invite on WhatsApp
He gets the time, a map link for a visit, and a way to move it.
note · Note
Add a note to Arjun Mehta

Notes are for your team. Veerha reads them; the guest never sees them.

assign · Assign owner
Assign Arjun Mehta

The new owner gets a notification, and Veerha introduces them by name if it replies next.

sequence · Put on a sequence
Put Arjun Mehta on a sequence
First step tomorrow at 10:00: a WhatsApp template, “Rooms for your dates”. It stops the moment he replies, books, or you take over.
confirm-archive · Archive lead
Archive Arjun Mehta?

The lead leaves every list and Veerha stops following up. Nothing is deleted: it can be restored from Leads › Archived, and a new message from them reopens it.

confirm-delete · Delete lead
Delete Arjun Mehta?

Their conversations, notes, follow-ups and score are removed. This cannot be undone — archive instead if you might need them.
