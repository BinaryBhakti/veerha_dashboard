22Front Desk — day /front-deskA10

The live board repeats the same 44-word disclaimer on all twelve cards, which is about 60% of the panel and says nothing about any guest. Here it is said once, and the room number — the only thing a front desk is actually looking for — is the largest element on the row. Both halves of status are shown together: who has arrived and whose booking we hold. One stay came through Veerha and carries everything; five came from the hotel's own system and carry a name, a room and dates.
