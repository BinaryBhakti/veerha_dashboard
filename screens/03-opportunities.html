4 Opportunity record drawer /opportunities?open=:id A3

Same A3 machine as the lead drawer — one header, one close — with the deal's own sections hung off it. Three raw-data leaks are fixed here, not styled around. The red overdue banner in the live app ships the literal string "The task was due ." with a null date and an orphaned full stop; it now carries the real date and says how late it is, and the follow-up that genuinely has no date says No date set in words. Source printed the database enum whatsapp_call; it now says what actually happened. The activity feed printed Stageproposal Sent and Stageconverted — a label glued to its value; label and value are now separate things. The stepper also stops mixing a tick, a dot and two numerals across four steps, and the destructive pair is grouped and guarded instead of sitting in the same list style as the reversible actions above it. In the walkthrough every deal row opens this drawer: the header and value follow the row; the body below is one drawn example deal.

4bOpportunity — dialogs dialogsopened from buttons on this module’s screens

Closing a deal either way asks one question that the live product never asks: why. Won creates the booking from the deal; Lost records a reason that Analytics can count and can keep the customer on a gentle sequence. The live drawer offers Archive and Delete with no confirmation; both get one here.

mark-won · Mark won
Mark the deal with Arjun Mehta as won
Create the booking now
Rooms, dates and guests come from the deal. The guest gets the stay page to check in online.

Won deals leave the pipeline and count towards Attribution for the channel they came from.

mark-lost · Mark lost
Why was the deal with Arjun Mehta lost?

The reason shows on Analytics, so lost deals teach something.

Keep them on a nurture sequence
A gentle note in three months, the season they travel.
confirm-archive-deal · Archive deal
Archive this deal?

It leaves the pipeline and its follow-ups stop. Nothing is deleted — the customer, the conversation and any quotation stay, and the deal can be restored.

confirm-delete-deal · Delete deal
Delete this deal?

The deal and its activity are removed; the customer and their conversations stay. This cannot be undone — archive instead to keep the history.
