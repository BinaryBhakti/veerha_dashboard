77 screens, 12 templates.

The product's 103 routes collapse to twelve layout archetypes plus a handful of genuinely one-off pages. That collapse is what makes this tractable: the templates are designed properly once, and most screens are then a column schema and some copy. Every screen below is drawn against the Indigo Slate design system — no screen introduces a colour, a radius or a component of its own.

Open the clickable walkthrough Browse the screens
Two ways to review this. The walkthrough is the product as you would use it — click the rail, open a lead, switch to the queue, hit ⌘K. The screen set below is the specification: every screen and every state laid out as flat frames, including the ones a walkthrough cannot show you because you would have to break something to reach them.

03

What still needs a decision

The guest-page blocker is closed — and it was partly ours. We reported these for weeks as waiting on share links from the client. Sweeping the captured conversation threads turned up three real stay-hub tokens that had been in the crawl all along, and a read-only pass inside the product found an enquiry-form link. So four of the ten are now drawn against the live pages, including the expired-link copy, which is quoted verbatim because it is the one piece of this wording the product already had right.
Two routes the contract names do not exist yet. /s/:token/review falls through to the marketing page, and /book/:token reports “No bookable people yet”. /q/ and /p/ render nothing because there is no data behind them, not because the screens are withheld. Those six are designed from settings/fields and settings/hours, and every frame note says which of the two it is.
Three screens are hiding the state that matters. The proposal builder has only ever rendered its empty basket, the org chart has every person unplaced so the tree has never drawn, and the booking detail exists only in the variant that deliberately shows nothing. Each is listed in ADD-ONS.md.
