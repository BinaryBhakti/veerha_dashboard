56 Dashboard — shift brief /dashboard A9 · bespoke

The first thing anyone sees. It answers the five questions in order: what happened, what Veerha did, why, what needs me, what next. The brief is a grouped section with ruled rows rather than a marketing hero — a dark full-bleed slab reads as a landing page, and this is software someone keeps open for eight hours. Reconciled to the live brief in October: Today and Analytics are tabs, and the live quick-action row — add a lead, message, schedule, quote, opportunity, task — sits under the brief.

60 Analytics & Control Room /analytics A9 · bespoke

Four charts, one series colour, no library. Every chart carries all four required slots — a context eyebrow, a plain-language title, a timeframe or denominator, and a takeaway: one bolded conclusion plus one sentence explaining it. A chart without a takeaway is a chart the reader has to interpret themselves, which is the work this page exists to do for them.
