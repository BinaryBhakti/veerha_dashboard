Departments

Four departments. Every request is routed to one of them by what it is about — 168 were routed last month.

A department decides who handles a request. It does not decide who can see one. Visibility follows reporting lines — a manager sees everyone reporting to them, and anybody without a manager sees only their own customers. That is set on the org chart, not here. Org chart › Reporting lines →
