Conversations

Where the product is most itself. Three panes — who is talking, what they said, and what Veerha knows about them — and one line that has to be unmistakable at all times: who is answering, the AI or you. Ambiguity there is the worst failure this product can have.

18 Inbox — Veerha handling /conversations A4 · three-pane

The default state: Veerha is answering and you are watching. The banner says so, and states the two conditions that would make it stop — so the boundary is visible before it is crossed, not after. AI messages are attributed in the footer, not turned into a differently coloured bubble.

19 Status & test /channels A5

Where the product's biggest current problem lives. A disconnected channel is not a settings row — it is seventeen of twenty-seven leads Veerha can read and cannot answer, so the cost is stated in the card. The live product now splits this from Connections (#79): this page is per channel — is it live, send a test, how fast to answer — and Connections holds the accounts and keys behind them. The four channels match live: WhatsApp, Email, Instagram & Messenger, Google Business Profile.
