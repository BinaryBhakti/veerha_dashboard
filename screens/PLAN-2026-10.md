## 1 · What the re-check established

**The live app now matches the sitemap's structure.** The sitemap was written from the product,
and the crawl confirms it row for row:

| Top tabs | Dashboard: *Today · Analytics* · Conversations: *Chat · Customer Mail · Callbacks · Google Reviews* · Customers: *Customers · Touchpoints* | Tabs exist as a component but Callbacks and Touchpoints live in Work queues, Mail is separate |

| Settings rail | 8 groups, ~36 items (below), plus a *Need help? Documentation · Support* card | 5 groups, ~17 items |

**Where live and sitemap differ** — worth confirming with the client:

- **Teach Veerha** (`/settings/teach/*`) and **Grow** (`/settings/grow`) are live and in the rail
  but **absent from the sitemap**.
- **`/oauth/zoom`** exists; Zoom is not in the sitemap's integrations list.
- **Credit notes** are in the sitemap's Billing row; live Billing shows no such section.
- **Edition-gated routes redirect to Leads** in a Hotels workspace: `/counter`, `/orders`,
  `/photo-studio`, `/catalogs/offers`. They cannot be observed without an E-commerce workspace.
