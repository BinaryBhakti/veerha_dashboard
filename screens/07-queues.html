Work queues

Four screens, one template. A2 is a stack of record cards with the action on the row — no opening, no drawer, no hunting. What changes between them is the verb on the button and the severity rail down the left edge; the structure does not change at all.

8Touchpoints /touchpointsA1 · the numbers reconcile

In the live app the channel columns never sum to TOTAL — one row reads 0 / 0 / 58 / 0 / 0 against a total of 77, and the KPI strip repeats it (0+0+126+11 = 137 against "Total touches 192"). Nothing discloses the gap, which makes every number on the screen unusable. Here the missing class is named — Other: notes, forms and qualification changes, logged interactions that are not messages — given its own column, its own KPI and its own line in the drawer. Channels plus Other equals the total on every row, and the summary is drawn as the sum itself.

8bTouchpoints — row drawer /touchpoints?open=A1 · row state

The row opens into the events behind its own numbers. The live drawer labels the same five channels a third way (Calls · Meets · WA · Email · Chat, against full words in the KPI strip and bare emoji in the table); here one vocabulary is used in all three places. Every event carries its class, so the six that make up Other can be read rather than inferred.
