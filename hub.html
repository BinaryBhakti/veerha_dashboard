Worth knowing

Four directions, live

In the dashboard, press 1 2 3 4 to switch between Warm Editorial, Cold Press, Bureau and Indigo Slate. Same screens, same data — only the visual language moves. Indigo Slate is the one that was signed off, and the one everything else here is built in.

Deep links

Add ?dir=d to open the dashboard directly in Indigo Slate (a–d for the four), and ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to pin the theme. A parameter overrides whatever the browser remembered, so a shared link lands where you meant it to.

The walkthrough

In the screen set, Walkthrough is the driveable version: one app shell, 45 screens, a rail and a ⌘K command palette. Click a lead row to open its drawer. Any screen is linkable by its hash.
