Foundations

The decisions everything else inherits. Change a value here and it moves everywhere it is used — which is the point. Nothing in the product should reach past these.

01

Colour

Four independent layers: neutrals carry the structure, brand carries action and selection, semantic carries meaning, and the AI layer marks what Veerha did. They do not borrow from each other. Brand is never applied simply to make the interface look branded.

Neutrals
slate

The canvas is a cool slate. Surfaces separate from it by a hairline border rather than by shadow — the product is mostly flat, and cards should not all appear to float.

The AI system layer
warm greige — not purple

Everything Veerha itself produced — a read, an inference, an action it took — sits on this layer. It is deliberately a warm neutral rather than a colour, because AI identity should come from the mark, the wording and the evidence, not from a hue.

Mark and icons
Why it is not violet. The earlier violet sat 19° from the brand hue at 83% saturation — measurably the same colour family as indigo, so AI surfaces did not read as distinct from ordinary brand chrome. This greige is 158° opposite at 11% saturation. Against a cool slate canvas the warmth is unmistakably deliberate, which is what a system layer wants.
Semantic
independent of brand

Four families, each with a background, border, text and icon value. Colour is never the only signal — every status also carries a label, and most carry an icon.

Do

Use colour to communicate action, status, risk and attention. Pair every semantic colour with a label so it still works without colour vision.

Don't

Don't tint a surface indigo to signal "this is important", and don't give AI a colour of its own beyond the greige layer. Importance is hierarchy, not hue.

02

Typography

Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for figures so columns of numbers align. Three weights — 400, 500, 600. There is no 700: hierarchy comes from scale and spacing before it comes from weight, and from weight before it comes from colour.

The typefaces
Geist · Geist Mono

Two faces, both self-hosted in system/fonts/ so the folder works with no network. Geist is an open-source family (SIL Open Font License 1.1) built for interfaces — its letterforms stay distinct at 12–14px, which is where most of this product lives.

Every digit occupies the same width, so the rupee column lines up on the decimal and a price can be compared by eye down the column. This is the entire reason for the second face — it is used for figures, never for prose.

Money is never mono. The mono face is for identifiers and bare figures — record codes, times, scores, durations. It is never for a formatted amount, anywhere: a monospace comma takes a full character cell, so ₹8,69,500 renders as ₹8 , 69 , 500, and Indian grouping makes it worse. Three components set mono on their numeric slot — .v-stat__n, .v-table__num and .v-kv dd — so put .amount on anything carrying a currency symbol and it returns to the UI face. That face already carries tabular numerals, which is the only alignment a money column needs.

Do

Set identifiers, times and scores in Geist Mono so columns align. Set money in the UI face with .amount — it is already tabular, and mono breaks the grouping commas.

Don't

No serif anywhere in the interface. "Premium" and "editorial" are principles about clarity and restraint — they are not instructions to add a serif face.

04

Radius & elevation

One radius per role, the same at every breakpoint. Fully-round is reserved for avatars, dots and compact semantic pills — the product is not pill-shaped. Elevation is mostly absent: surfaces separate by border, and shadow is reserved for things that genuinely float.

chip · 4px
control · 6px
tile · 10px
card · 12px
full
Reconciled from the prototype. Indigo Slate declared a 16px card radius but only ever applied it on mobile — every desktop surface was actually 10px, so the card changed shape between breakpoints for no reason. One value per role now, identical at every width.

05

Icons & motion

One icon family — Lucide, consistent stroke, never mixed with another set. Motion is short and functional: it explains where something came from, and then gets out of the way.
