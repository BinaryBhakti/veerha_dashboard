Signature patterns

Six compositions that should become more recognisable than the colour palette. They are how the product explains itself: what Veerha concluded, what it suggests, why it believes that, what it has been doing, what it needs from you, and who did the work. Everything else in this system exists to support them.

00

The order that governs them

Every AI surface follows the same progression — insight, then evidence, then action. A conclusion with no evidence is a guess the user has no way to check; evidence with no action is homework. Reversing this order is the most common way these patterns fail.

01

Veerha's Read

The interpretation. A conclusion in plain language, the evidence behind it, the attributes it inferred, and what it was based on. The intelligence should be obvious from the content — never from the styling.

VVeerha's read
Ready for a callback, not another message.

Arjun asked for a callback about a two-adult booking for 15–17 October and has already chosen the Premier River Retreat room. He has replied within the hour every time.

High intent · callback requested · price sensitive
Based on: 4 replies · confirmed dates · 10 previous enquiries
PartRule
EyebrowAlways VEERHA'S READ, with the mark. Never a sparkle icon.
ConclusionOne sentence, and it must commit to something. "Ready for a callback, not another message" — not "This lead shows high engagement."
EvidenceTwo or three sentences of the actual facts, in the customer's terms.
AttributesInferred qualities, separated by ·. These are conclusions, not data fields.
Based onThe provenance line. What the read was computed from, so it can be doubted.
Do

Write the conclusion the way a good colleague would say it across a desk.

Don't

Don't make this a purple AI card with a glow. The greige layer and the wording carry it; decoration would only make it look less trustworthy.

02

Recommended Next Step

The action. One of the most important components in the product: it is the thing that turns a read into work done. It carries a time, a justification, and exactly two buttons.

Recommended next step
Call Arjun tomorrow at 11:00

He asked for it, and has replied within the hour every time.

Two buttons, never more. One does it now, one defers it. A third option turns a recommendation back into a decision, which is the work this pattern exists to remove. This is the one place brand indigo tints a surface — because it is the single most actionable thing on the screen.
03

Evidence

The reasoning, as a ledger. Each row is a claim and where it came from, and every source is a link — the user can always go and check. Crucially it includes the gaps, not only the confirmations.

Do

Include what's missing. "Nothing scheduled yet" is why the recommendation exists — hiding it makes the read look like magic instead of reasoning.

Don't

Don't list raw field values. "source: whatsapp" is data; "Replied 4 times, always within the hour" is evidence.

Veerha Is Working

Autonomous activity, as a timeline: time, what it did, and what changed. This is what makes the product feel alive — without a single animation.

Human handoff

The states a conversation moves between, and the one line that must appear when a person takes over. This has to be unmistakable — ambiguity about who is replying is the worst failure this product can have.
