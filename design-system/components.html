Components

Every state drawn as its own frame — default, hover, focus, selected, disabled, loading, error. Nothing is hidden behind an interaction, so the whole library can be reviewed by scrolling. Variants over duplicates: a component with six looks is one component.

01

The app shell

Every internal screen sits inside this. An icon rail for modules, a quiet top bar, and — on the config-heavy areas — a second-level section nav inside the canvas. The rail is the only navigation that never changes.

.v-rail

56px. Workspace mark at the top, modules below, settings pinned to the bottom. The active item takes a tinted chip and a 2px rule at the rail's edge — colour is never the only signal. A red dot marks a module with work waiting in it.

Why it is here and not above the content

In the live product this nav renders inline above the page on narrow viewports. On the Settings tree that is roughly 27 items — about 1,400px — so more than half the app opens on a phone showing nothing but a menu. Keeping it a sibling of the content, not a block before it, is what makes the responsive pass possible later.

Buttons

One dominant primary action per section. If everything is primary, nothing is — secondary and ghost exist so the important action stays obvious. Danger comes in two weights: solid when destroying the thing is the point of the screen, quiet when it is a way out that has to be findable without being the loudest thing on the page — a guest cancelling a call, not an admin deleting a workflow.

07

Feedback

An empty state answers three questions: what is empty, why, and what should I do. An error answers the same three. Neither is ever a shrug.

Empty state
.v-empty
No leads waiting to be qualified

Everything that came in has been handled. New enquiries from your connected channels will appear here as they arrive.

Error
.v-error
WhatsApp could not be connected

Your credentials expired on 18 September. Eleven of seventeen leads arrive on this channel.

Do

Name what broke, say why, and give the button that fixes it.

Don't

"Something went wrong." "Not authorized." Both appear in the product today, and neither tells anyone anything.

Save state
.v-savebar

Today this bar is pinned bottom-right on every settings screen and sits on top of the content beneath it. It belongs in the page footer, in flow, where it cannot cover anything.

08

Overlays

Drawers preserve context — you can still see the list you came from. Modals interrupt, so they are reserved for confirmation and destructive actions.

Confirm dialog
.v-modal

No confirmation dialog exists in the product today, yet destructive buttons do. This is the pattern for them: name the consequence, and make the destructive verb the one on the button.

Delete this workflow?

“Overdue follow-up nudge” has run 231 times and is currently active for 27 leads. Deleting it stops those follow-ups immediately. This cannot be undone.

Do

Label the action with the verb, not "OK". Say what is lost and whether it is recoverable.

Don't

Don't use a modal for anything reversible. A toast with undo is less interruptive and usually kinder.

Actions — labelled action, menu, tooltip
.v-action · .v-menu · [data-tip]

An action that changes a record says what it does on its face. The live app hangs WhatsApp, Call, Email, Archive and Convert on unlabelled icons with the meaning in a tooltip; here the word is always there, and the ··· menu holds the rest as verbs, destructive ones last. A tooltip is kept for chrome that is genuinely icon-only.

Charts

Hand-authored inline SVG. No library, no runtime, and one series colour — a chart that needs to distinguish more than one series is usually two charts.

The four required slots
.g-*

A chart is not finished until it has all four. The takeaway is the one that gets skipped and the one that matters — without it the reader has to interpret the picture themselves, which is the work the page exists to do for them.

SlotWhat goes in it
.labelContext eyebrow — Volume · Autonomy · Conversion · Sources
h3Plain-language title. "Where leads stop", not "Funnel analysis"
.v-card__descTimeframe or denominator — "84 leads captured, last 30 days"

role="img"An aria-label stating the data as a sentence. An SVG of a line is nothing at all to a screen reader.

Do

Say what the chart means. "The drop is at qualification. 45 leads never gave enough to qualify."

Don't

No 3D, no decorative pie charts, no gradients for their own sake, and never more than one series colour.
